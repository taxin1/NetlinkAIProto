## 📧 Tab 1: Individual Emails

### Features:
1. **✨ AI Email Generation**
   - Click "Compose Email" button
   - Select a contact from your list
   - Enter the purpose (e.g., "Schedule a meeting")
   - AI generates a personalized email automatically!
   - Edit and customize before sending

2. **📝 Draft Management**
   - Save emails as drafts
   - Edit drafts anytime
   - Send when ready

3. **📤 Send Emails**
   - Send individual personalized emails
   - Track sent/draft status
   - View email history

### How to Use:
\`\`\`
1. Go to Dashboard → Emails
2. Click "Compose Email" button
3. Select a contact
4. Enter purpose: "Follow up on our meeting"
5. Click "Generate" - AI creates the email!
6. Edit if needed
7. Click "Send Email" or "Save Draft"
\`\`\`

## 🤖 Tab 2: AI Campaigns

### Features:
1. **Campaign Creation**
   - Create email campaigns with multiple recipients
   - Set campaign name, purpose, and subject
   - Select multiple contacts at once
   - AI generates unique emails for each person!

2. **Automated Sending**
   - Click "Run" to start the campaign
   - AI personalizes each email based on contact details
   - Sends automatically with delays to avoid spam
   - Real-time progress tracking

3. **Campaign Management**
   - View all campaigns (draft, running, completed)
   - Track sent count vs total
   - See campaign status
   - Monitor progress with progress bars

### How to Use:
\`\`\`
1. Go to Dashboard → Emails
2. Click "AI Campaigns" tab
3. Fill in campaign details:
   - Name: "Q1 Outreach"
   - Purpose: "Introduce our new product"
   - Subject: "Exciting news about [product]"
4. Select contacts (check multiple boxes)
5. Click "Create Campaign"
6. Click "Run" to start sending
7. Watch the progress bar!
\`\`\`

## 🔧 Technical Details

### AI Generation:
- **Model**: Gemini 2.5 Flash
- **Personalization**: Uses contact name, company, position
- **Context**: Considers your purpose and contact history
- **Safety**: Reviews content before sending

### Email Sending:
- **Method**: SMTP (Gmail or custom)
- **Rate Limiting**: 1-second delay between emails
- **Status Tracking**: Real-time updates in database
- **Error Handling**: Failed sends logged separately

### Data Storage:
- **Emails Table**: All sent/draft emails
- **Campaigns Table**: Campaign metadata
- **Campaign_Contacts**: Contact-campaign relationships
- **Events Log**: Send history for analytics
