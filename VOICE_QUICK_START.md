## 🎛️ Controls

### Microphone Button 🎤
- **Click once** = Start listening
- **Click again** = Stop listening
- **Pulsing blue** = Currently listening
- **Gray** = Ready to listen

### Volume Button 🔊
- **On** = AI speaks responses aloud
- **Off** = AI only shows text responses

### Help Button ❓
- Click to see voice command examples

---

## ⚡ Pro Tips

1. **Be Specific**: Instead of "send email", say "send email to john@example.com"
2. **Wait for Badge**: Look for "Listening..." before speaking
3. **One Command at a Time**: Finish one command before starting another
4. **Confirm Actions**: Click "Confirm & Send" for emails
5. **Check Live Transcript**: See your words appear in real-time

---

## 🎯 Example Workflow

### Sending an Email by Voice:

1. Click 🎤 microphone button
2. Say: **"Send email to sarah@company.com about project update"**
3. Wait for AI response (spoken + displayed)
4. Review the email details
5. Click **"Confirm & Send"**
6. Done! ✅

## 🐛 Quick Troubleshooting

**Mic not working?**
→ Check browser permissions (Chrome/Edge recommended)

**Command not understood?**
→ Speak slower and clearer, add more details

**No sound?**
→ Click the 🔊 button to enable voice responses
